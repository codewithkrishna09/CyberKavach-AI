Chapter 01 Binary Forensics

The IndiaPost SMS Trojan: Decompiling the Intent

The "Package Delivery" scam has evolved. Scammers no longer ask you to enter your credit card details on a website. Instead, they send an SMS appearing to be from IndiaPost, containing a link to download a "tracking app" (an .apk file).

The Fatal Mistake: Once installed, the app asks for permissions. Most users instinctively click "Allow" without reading. The moment this happens, the device is compromised.

The Smali Code Deconstruction

When you upload a suspicious APK to CyberKavach's APK Shield, we don't just scan its hash against a database. We decompile the compiled Java bytecode back into Smali code to inspect its actual behavior. Here is an actual snippet recovered from a fake IndiaPost APK by our engine:

AndroidManifest.xml 
<manifest xmlns:android="http://schemas.android.com/apk/res/android">
  <!-- The Silent Killers -->
  <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS"/>
  <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.READ_SMS"/>

  <receiver android:name=".SmsInterceptor">
    <intent-filter android:priority="999">
      <action android:name="android.provider.Telephony.SMS_RECEIVED"/>
    </intent-filter>
  </receiver>
</manifest>

Notice the android:priority="999" tag. This tells the Android OS: "When an SMS arrives, send it to this app FIRST, before the default messaging app."

The hacker's server initiates a login to your bank account. Your bank sends an OTP. The malicious app intercepts the OTP, forwards it to the hacker's Telegram Bot via the internet, and deletes the SMS from your phone. Your account is drained, and you never even heard a notification sound.

Chapter 02 Social Engineering

The Screen-Share Epidemic: Weaponizing Trust

Financial fraud is increasingly moving away from pure software exploitation to psychology. The most devastating vector currently operational in India is the "Remote Access" scam. Apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer are legitimate tools built for IT support. Because they are legal, no traditional Antivirus will flag them as malicious.

The Attack Flow

Scammers manipulate Google Ads to show fraudulent "Customer Care" numbers at the top of search results. When a victim calls, the fake executive asks them to download a legitimate app from the Play Store to "fix their issue".

The Psychological Blindspot

Victims fundamentally misunderstand how UPI works. Scammers use the "Collect Request" feature and tell the victim: "Enter your PIN to RECEIVE the refund money." Fact: You never need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money. PINs are strictly for deductions.

Because the app is legitimate, the victim shares the 9-digit pairing code. As the victim enters their UPI PIN for a "test transaction", the scammer watches it live on their screen. Within seconds, the balance is wiped out.

CyberKavach addresses this via Behavioral Context. If our engine detects a remote-desktop application casting the screen while a banking application is opened in the foreground, it instantly freezes the screen to break the scammer's visual feed.

Chapter 03 AI Deepfakes & ELA

Digital Arrests: Deepfakes & PDF Forensics

The "Digital Arrest" is a purely psychological terror campaign. Victims (often senior citizens) receive a Skype video call from individuals dressed in police uniforms, sitting in setups mimicking a CBI or Customs office.

Deconstructing the "Official" Warrant

The scammers send a PDF warrant carrying the Ashoka Pillar, official seals, and the victim's Aadhaar details. To a terrified citizen, it looks 100% real. To a machine, it is a glaring forgery.

When processed through Satark AI, we apply Error Level Analysis (ELA). When a stamp is copied from Google and pasted onto a PDF document using Photoshop, the compression level of the stamp is different from the rest of the document.

ELA highlights this difference mathematically. The forged stamp glows bright white on our backend radar, instantly proving the document is a fabrication.

Catching the Deepfake Voice

Scammers also clone voices of family members using Generative AI. While humans hear a seamless sentence, the AI leaves micro-gaps (spectral discontinuities) between syllables.

Satark AI runs a Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) on the audio file. If the spectral cadence lacks natural human breath patterns and exhibits mathematical perfection, our engine flags it with a 98% probability of being synthetically generated.

Chapter 04 Web Exploitation

Zero-Day Phishing & Cloud Abuse

The era of buying shady domains is over. Modern phishers exploit legitimate, free Developer Platforms like Vercel, Netlify, and Firebase to host their credential harvesters. Traditional AVs cannot block an entire vercel.app domain without breaking millions of legitimate sites.

The Captcha Evasion Tactic

To prevent security bots from analyzing their fake code, hackers place a legitimate Cloudflare "Turnstile" or reCAPTCHA before the fake bank login. A Google Safe Browsing bot hits the CAPTCHA, cannot solve it, and assumes the site is safe.

Once a human solves the CAPTCHA, the server sends a massively encrypted, obfuscated JavaScript payload using AES-256. To any network scanner, it looks like harmless gibberish.

Encrypted_Payload.js 
var _0x4b2a = "U2FsdGVkX1+QJk...[10k chars of Base64]...aZ9b=";
var k = prompt("Verify Access"); // Secret from CAPTCHA

// Decrypts payload only inside the victim's browser
document.write( CryptoJS.AES.decrypt(_0x4b2a, k).toString(CryptoJS.enc.Utf8) );
