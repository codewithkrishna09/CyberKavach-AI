The Anatomy of Darknet Monitoring

Hackers don't just guess passwords; they buy them in bulk. When a popular app or website is compromised, billions of user details are dumped onto hidden forums. Shadow Scout acts as a periscope into these realms.

K-Anonymity Protocol

The Risk: Typing your password into a random website to check if it's leaked is dangerous. They might log it.

Our Solution: Shadow Scout uses SHA-1 Hashing. We never send your actual password to the server. We convert it to a hash and only check a fragment of it, matching the rest securely on your own device.

UPI VPA Forensics

The Scam: You are buying a second-hand phone online. The seller gives you a UPI ID (e.g., ravi@oksbi). How do you know they aren't a fraudster?

Our Solution: Entering a UPI VPA into Shadow Scout instantly cross-references it with community fraud registries and cybercrime blacklists, warning you before you make that payment.

The RockYou Archive

The Intelligence: In 2024, the largest compilation of stolen passwords was uploaded to a hacker forum, containing nearly 10 Billion plaintext records.

Our Network: Our API gateway is hooked directly into threat intelligence feeds. We scan your query across data breaches from major companies to tell you exactly if your credentials have been compromised.

Deep Dive: APK Kavach Architecture

APK Kavach goes beyond basic signature matching. It performs deep static binary analysis, decompiling Android packages to their core to detect obfuscated malware and unauthorized intents.

Binary Forensics

Smali Bytecode Decompilation

Scammers hide their malicious code by compiling it. When you upload an Android Package (.apk), APK Kavach extracts the classes.dex file and decompiles it into human-readable Smali bytecode.

Our engine parses this code line-by-line, actively hunting for dangerous Android Intents like android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS or BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE, which are the primary vectors used to steal OTPs and take over device screens.

  • Parses obfuscated and minified Android code.
  • Extracts hardcoded hacker server URLs and API keys.

Neural Matching

Heuristic Signature Matching

Modern malware mutates. A scammer might change the icon or the app name from "IndiaPost" to "Electricity Board," but the core malicious algorithm remains mathematically similar.

Our engine uses fuzzy hashing and heuristic analysis to compare the uploaded APK against a vast, continuously updated database of over 1.2 million known banking trojans and spyware active in the Indian subcontinent.

Infrastructure

Volatile RAM Processing

Decompiling large APK files requires massive computational power. APK Kavach relies on a distributed cloud architecture to process heavy files in under 2 seconds.

When you upload an APK, it is transmitted via a 256-bit encrypted pipeline. The file is unpacked and analyzed entirely in volatile RAM. Once the forensic report is generated and sent to your dashboard, the APK is instantly purged from our systems.

Absolute Privacy: We do not keep copies of your uploaded apps. We extract the threat intelligence and immediately destroy the binary payload.
