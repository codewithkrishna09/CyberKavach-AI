    Why a Browser Extension?

    Websites load in milliseconds. If we send every URL to a distant cloud server for analysis, the malicious script has already executed by the time the warning arrives. The browser is the ultimate endpoint.

    Pre-DOM Interception

    The extension blocks malicious IFrames and obfuscated JavaScript in your device's memory, preventing the browser from even rendering the fake page visually.

    Drive-By Download Blocks

    Instantly severs the HTTP connection if a website attempts to force-download an invisible .exe or .apk payload in the background.

    Absolute Anonymity

    Scanning occurs completely locally via the OpsCenter Engine. We do not track your browsing history or log the URLs you visit. True Zero-Knowledge architecture.

    Why 100% Security
    is a Myth.

    If a cybersecurity vendor promises you absolute 100% protection, they are selling you snake oil. The cyber landscape is an endless cat-and-mouse game. Hackers continuously invent novel "Zero-Day" payloads and use Generative AI to bypass filters.

    Our AI is probabilistic, not deterministic. It analyzes DOM patterns, pixel structures, and spectral anomalies to catch what traditional antivirus misses. We act as a formidable augmented shield—but human common sense remains the ultimate firewall.

    Accuracy 0%

    The remaining 0.8% accounts for unprecedented zero-day adversarial attacks.

    Zero-Day Phishing

    Scammers launch thousands of new domains daily. They harvest data and destroy the site within hours, long before global blacklists (like Google Safe Browsing) can even detect them.

    Adversarial AI

    Hackers now use machine learning to test their own deepfakes against detection algorithms. They constantly mutate the malicious payload until it learns how to slip through the system.

    The Human Exploit

    No line of code can stop a user from willingly transferring money out of panic. Social engineering targets human psychology directly, making it the hardest vulnerability to patch.
