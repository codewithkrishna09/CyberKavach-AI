The Anatomy of Darknet Monitoring

Hackers don't just guess passwords; they buy them in bulk. When a popular app or website is compromised, billions of user details are dumped onto hidden forums. Shadow Scout acts as a periscope into these realms.

K-Anonymity Protocol

The Risk: Typing your password into a random website to check if it's leaked is dangerous. They might log it.

Our Solution: Shadow Scout uses SHA-1 Hashing. We never send your actual password to the server. We convert it to a hash and only check a fragment of it, matching the rest securely on your own device.

UPI VPA Forensics

The Scam: You are buying a second-hand phone online. The seller gives you a UPI ID (e.g., ravi@oksbi). How do you know they aren't a fraudster?

Our Solution: Entering a UPI VPA into Shadow Scout instantly cross-references it with community fraud registries and cybercrime blacklists, warning you before you make that payment.

Deep Dive: Shadow Scout Capabilities

Shadow Scout isn't just a simple database lookup. It's a localized, privacy-first forensic tool built specifically for the modern Indian digital threat ecosystem.

Mathematical Privacy

Zero-Knowledge Payload Hashing

When you enter a password to check for leaks, transmitting it in plaintext is a severe security risk. Shadow Scout computes the SHA-1 Hash of your string locally on your device before any network request is made.

We only transmit the first 5 characters of this hash to our secure nodes. The server returns a list of hundreds of matching hashes, and the final exact-match comparison happens locally in your browser memory.

  • The CyberKavach server never sees your actual password.
  • Immune to MITM (Man-in-the-Middle) interception attacks.
India-Centric Security

UPI Fraud Registry Cross-Check

The Indian financial ecosystem is fundamentally different from the West. Cyber-syndicates abuse the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) using "collect-request" manipulation and fake OLX/Quikr buyer personas.

Shadow Scout maintains an active, continuously updated ledger of flagged UPI VPAs (Virtual Payment Addresses). This ledger is aggregated from crowd-sourced scam reports, cyber-cell warnings, and known phishing domain operators.

Real-World Use Case: Before transferring money to an unknown seller online, enter their UPI ID into Shadow Scout to instantly verify if they have a history of digital extortion.
