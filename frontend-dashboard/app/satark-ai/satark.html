Defeating Next-Gen Scams in India

Hackers are no longer just sending suspicious links; they are using psychological manipulation, AI voice cloning, and fabricated legal documents to extort money. Satark AI acts as your personal digital forensics lab to instantly verify the truth behind the media you receive.

Digital Arrest Forgery

The Scam: You receive a WhatsApp message containing a "CBI or Supreme Court Warrant" claiming you are implicated in a money laundering case. They demand an immediate "security deposit" to avoid arrest.

Satark Defense: Our engine runs Error Level Analysis (ELA) on the uploaded image. We detect mismatched pixels, unnatural font overlays, and missing digital signatures that prove the document was photoshopped.

Deepfake Voice Clones

The Scam: You get a frantic phone call or voice note that sounds exactly like your son, daughter, or friend, claiming they had an accident and urgently need ₹20,000 sent via UPI.

Satark Defense: By uploading the audio clip, Satark AI analyzes the sound's frequency spectrum. Human voices have natural breathing pauses. AI clones have "Spectral Flatness". We catch this in seconds.

Quishing & Steganography

The Scam: You receive a QR code via email promising a "Free Paytm Cashback". Alternatively, you download an innocent-looking picture that secretly contains malicious code.

Satark Defense: Our engine decodes the QR matrix *without* opening the link. It checks the destination URL. Furthermore, it scans the Least Significant Bits (LSB) of images to extract hidden payloads.

Deep Dive: Satark AI Forensic Core

Satark AI combines file metadata, document structure, QR decoding and conservative integrity checks. Every report explains what was found and what remains inconclusive.

Pixel-Level Forensics

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

When scammers forge a "Digital Arrest Warrant," they typically copy official CBI or Police logos and paste them onto a fake document. This process leaves a digital footprint.

Our backend runs an Error Level Analysis algorithm. It intentionally re-compresses the uploaded image to identify areas with differing compression levels. The pasted logo will glow brightly on our forensic radar compared to the rest of the document.

  • Exposes cloned signatures and forged official stamps.
  • Extracts hidden EXIF metadata (GPS, Device info) instantly.
Biometric Analysis

Spectral Flatness & Voice Cloning

AI-generated voices (Deepfakes) sound incredibly realistic to the human ear. However, generative algorithms struggle to replicate the natural micro-pauses, breathing patterns, and organic frequency variance of an actual human throat.

Satark AI currently checks audio container integrity and available metadata. A trained deepfake model can be added later only after it has been validated on a suitable audio dataset.

Infrastructure

Edge-to-Cloud Neural Sync

File analysis runs in the configured FastAPI backend so browser pages remain responsive. File size limits and type validation are applied before forensic processing begins.

The current application reads each upload for the scan and returns a forensic JSON report. Deployments should use HTTPS and define their own retention policy before accepting real user evidence.

Privacy note: Do not upload OTPs, identity documents or private recordings unless you have permission. Metadata can be sensitive and is shown carefully in the report.
