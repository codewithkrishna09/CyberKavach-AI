1. The Zero-Data Manifesto

Most cybersecurity companies operate on a flawed business model: they monitor your traffic to protect you, but simultaneously log your digital footprint to train their proprietary AI models or sell telemetry data to third-party brokers. At CyberKavach, we believe this fundamentally violates user sovereignty.

This submission build is a security research platform, not a payment service. It processes threats locally or ephemerally and does not request payment details or sell access keys.

2. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 Alignment

Designed natively for the Indian demographic, our architecture strictly adheres to the mandates set forth by the DPDP Act of 2023 by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).

  • Notice and Consent: You are explicitly informed when an external server call is required (e.g., uploading a CBI warrant to Satark AI). We do not run background telemetry without your active invocation.
  • Data Minimization: We do not request payment details or delivery emails. Local session identifiers are stored as one-way hashes for scan-history separation.
  • Data Deletion: Any digital artifact (Audio, Video, APK, or Image) uploaded to our API nodes is processed entirely in the Volatile Memory (RAM). It is cryptographically shredded the millisecond a verdict is generated.
  • Data Principal Rights: As an Indian citizen, you hold the absolute right to erasure. By clicking "Terminate Session" in your extension, your local identity is permanently revoked.

3. Technical Mechanics by Module

To provide complete transparency, here is the exact data flow for each of our Titan AI modules:

A. Web Interceptor (Browser Extension)

The extension analyzes the Document Object Model (DOM) directly inside your local Chrome/Edge environment. When checking a suspicious URL against our global threat database, the URL is hashed on your machine before transmission. We do not know *which* specific page you are visiting, only that a mathematical hash requested a threat lookup.

B. Shadow Scout (Dark Web Node)

When checking if your password or UPI ID has been leaked, we utilize the K-Anonymity Protocol via secure SHA-1 hashing. If your password is "bharat@123", it converts to E9A5B.... We only transmit the first 5 characters (E9A5B) to the backend. The server returns thousands of potential matches, and the final verification happens locally on your computer. Your real password never traverses the internet.

C. Satark AI & APK Kavach

Files (Images, Audio, .APK) are transmitted over TLS 1.3 (bank-grade encryption). The FastAPI master node loads the file into a temporary virtual sandbox. Once the heuristics (Smali decompilation or ELA analysis) are complete, the sandbox is destroyed. We do not use your uploaded evidence to train our Machine Learning models.

4. Financial Data & RBI Compliance

CyberKavach does not include a payment gateway in this submission build. We do not collect, process, or store card, UPI, netbanking, or payment-token details.

5. Law Enforcement & Grievance Redressal

As an entity operating under Indian jurisdiction, we comply fully with the directives of CERT-In (Computer Emergency Response Team - India) and lawful court orders. However, because our architecture is strictly "Zero-Logs", we cannot surrender browsing histories, decrypted passwords, or scanned files to law enforcement, simply because that data does not exist on our servers.
